Privacy Policy

123 Words keeps everything on your device.

Home Support Privacy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Summary: 123 Words does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app works entirely offline. No accounts, no analytics, no ads.

What we collect

Nothing. 123 Words has no servers, no analytics SDK, no advertising network, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

What stays on your device

  • Your selected voice — stored in your device's local preferences. Never transmitted anywhere.
  • Word list — bundled in the app. No downloads, no syncing.

Speech synthesis

123 Words uses Apple's built-in text-to-speech system (AVSpeechSynthesizer) to read and spell words. Speech processing happens entirely on your device using downloaded voice files. No audio is sent to any server.

Children's privacy

123 Words is designed for young children. We do not collect any personal information from any user of any age. The app qualifies for Apple's Kids category. No account creation is required or possible.

Internet access

123 Words does not require or use an internet connection. There are no network requests of any kind.

Data deletion

To remove all app data, delete the app from your device. iOS will erase the app's local storage automatically.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
