A joyful learning playground for little racers 🏁

Download on the App Store

🏁 Race! mode

Pick a one- or two-minute race, then swipe between five mini-games racing the same clock and scoring into the same total. Kids chase points in whichever games they're best at — Spell, Scramble, Memory, Listen & Pick, or Count It. Time's up? Stars at 10, 20, and 30 points. Track personal bests per duration and try to beat them.

📖 Practice mode

A separate, untimed reader. Swipe through 220+ short words — the app says each one aloud, then lights up the letters one by one as it spells them out. Every word has its own picture. Perfect for pre-readers learning at their own pace.

Five mini-games, one race

  • 🔤 Spell It — see the picture, tap the letters in order
  • 🔀 Word Scramble — unscramble the jumbled letters
  • 🔈 Listen & Pick — hear a word, pick the matching picture
  • 🃏 Memory Match — match words with their pictures (8 or 16 pairs)
  • ➕ Count It — friendly addition and subtraction with counting dots

Made for little hands

  • 220+ short words, chosen for clarity and child-friendliness
  • Giant colorful letter tiles that bounce and glow
  • Bright backgrounds that change with every swipe
  • 20+ English voices — let kids pick their favorite!
  • No ads · No in-app purchases · No internet needed

For parents

A hidden parental settings sheet (long-press the small version label at the bottom of the home screen) lets parents switch between Race! mode (new default) and Classic mode (the original per-game hub with no timer), reset best scores, and tune which math operations show up in Count It.

Great for

  • 🧒 Pre-readers aged 2–5 hearing their first words
  • 📖 Early readers building phonics and spelling skills
  • 👴👵 Grandparents playing and racing with grandchildren
  • 👯 Siblings competing for the high score
  • 🌍 ESL learners hearing clear English pronunciation
